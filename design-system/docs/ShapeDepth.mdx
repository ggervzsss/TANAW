## Shadows

Records rest flat on the page. A shadow is earned by floating above other content, not by being important (**The Paper Rule**).

## Spacing

A 4px base. Gaps of 8, 12 and 16px inside components, 16–24px between cards, 32px between page regions. Controls are 42px tall (buttons) and 40px (inputs); small variants are 32–34px.

## Motion

Entrances are short and calm (140–180ms, ease-out) and use the presets in `src/lib/motion.ts` (`fadeInDown`, `popIn`, `dialogIn`). Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
